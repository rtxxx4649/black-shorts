Star Wars Zero Company

Star Wars Zero Company is a single-player, turn-based tactics game set during the twilight of the Clone Wars. Fields of Mistria is a farming and life-simulation RPG blending farming, exploration, crafting, romance, and adventure. Players restore an overgrown farm while helping rebuild the village of Mistria after a devastating earthquake. The game also features magic, mining, fishing, ranching, character customization, seasonal festivals, and major progression and relationship features in its 1.0 release.

Beast of Reincarnation

Beast of Reincarnation is an action RPG set in a post-apocalyptic Japan in the year 4026. Players control Emma and her companion Koo, combining sword combat with commands that direct Koo's abilities. Madden NFL 27

Madden NFL 27 is an American football simulation focused on gameplay, Franchise management, Superstar progression, and Ultimate Team. The Persona Engine gives NFL players distinct personalities that influence their motivations and reactions in Franchise mode. Gameplay adds more physical receiver and defensive-back interactions, improved short-yardage mechanics, and new strategic pre-play controls, while Superstar introduces a G.O.A.T. Career Journey.
